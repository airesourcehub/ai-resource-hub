GPU priority seat (override)

By default the GPU is open to every signed-in member — jobs simply queue and run one at a time. Assigning the priority seat below reserves the whole GPU for one person: only their jobs run, and everyone else is paused until you clear it. Use it to jump the line for yourself or push a specific member’s project through. Leave it cleared for normal open access.
