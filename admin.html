GPU priority seat (override)

Your GPU is private: only you and the members you approve in GPU Access can use it (everyone else connects their own PC). Their jobs queue and run one at a time. Assigning the priority seat below reserves the whole GPU for one person: only their jobs run, and everyone else waits until you clear it.

GPU access

Sign-ups & GPU

Menu button & status

Influencer page order

Arrange the preset characters on the AI Influencer page. Drag a card to move it, or use the arrows. Visitors see them in this order, left to right, row by row (four across on a laptop). Untick Show to hide one. Click Save and everyone sees it on their next visit.

Motion presets

Clips people can drop their influencer into on the AI Influencer page (Explore → Motion Presets, and inside each character's popup). Use short clips, 4–15 seconds, of motion you have the rights to. Changes go live for everyone when you save.

Clothing guard

Off: every render shows exactly what was prompted. On: the render server adds the words below to every image and video prompt, so a character stays dressed when the prompt doesn't say what they're wearing. Applies to all users and takes effect within 30 seconds of saving.

Trained character @handles

Only trained characters get an @handle. It's how everyone mentions them in Scene mode and in Claude/ChatGPT (e.g. @CrissP). Untrained stock influencers stay plain cards (Recreate or drag into Motion). Users' own influencers can't take a trained handle. Their handles only need to be unique inside their own account, so two people can both have an @Chris. If you give a trained character a handle a user already has, the user's copy is freed and they pick a new one.
