Motion Transfer

Bring a still character to life. Upload a reference image of your subject and a driving video of the motion you want, and WAN 2.2 Animate makes your character perform that motion — full-body pose and facial expression — on our own hardware.

Animate a character from a video

The subject in your reference image copies the movement and expressions of the person in the driving video, keeping your character’s identity.

Tap to add music-video style directions to your prompt — they stack.
